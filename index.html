1Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the ExamPro mobile application ("ExamPro", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the app you agree to the practices described below.

2Information stored on your device

ExamPro stores the content you create or import — for example study cards, questions, answers, notes, attached photos, voice notes and imported documents — as well as your preferences and progress, in the app's storage on your device.

This information is not uploaded to our servers.

3Device permissions

The app asks for access to a feature only when you use it. You can grant or revoke every permission at any time in iOS Settings.

CameraTake photos of exam materials, textbooks and notes and attach them to cards.
Photo LibraryAttach photos to cards and save card images to your library.
MicrophoneRecord voice notes and audio explanations. Recordings stay on your device.
LocationRemember where you study and see your most productive spots. Stored on your device only.
ContactsPick classmates or study-group members when sharing. Never uploaded to our servers.
CalendarFind free time slots and create study sessions, reminders and exam deadlines.
BluetoothShare study materials directly with nearby devices.
Face IDLock the app and protect private content. Biometric data is handled by iOS and never reaches the app.
NotificationsDeliver study reminders and calls from in-app features.

4AI features and third-party services

When you use the AI feature that simplifies an answer, the text of that answer (and the language you selected) is sent over an encrypted connection to a third-party AI provider (Groq, running a Llama language model) to generate the simplified version. The provider processes the request under its own privacy policy.

We do not intentionally send your name, contacts, location or other personal data as part of this request.

Tip: please don't enter sensitive personal information into content you send to the AI feature.

5Analytics

The app does not use third-party advertising or behavioural analytics services. We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA).

6Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Data leaves your device only when you explicitly use a feature that requires it — for example the AI feature described above, or sharing materials with nearby users via Bluetooth — or if required by law.

7Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. You can delete individual items inside the app, or delete all app data by uninstalling ExamPro. Removing the app deletes the locally stored content.

8Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including encrypted network connections for features that use the network and an optional biometric lock for the app. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9Children's privacy

ExamPro is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Since we do not maintain user accounts or store your content on our servers, most of these actions can be performed directly within the app or through your device settings. For any request, contact us using the details below.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
